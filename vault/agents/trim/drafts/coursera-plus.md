## How to cancel

Coursera cancels in account settings, no email needed:

1. Go to coursera.org and log in.
2. Click your profile photo, top right, then Settings.
3. Scroll to My Purchases, find Coursera Plus, click Manage Subscription.
4. Click Cancel Subscription and confirm.
5. The page should say the subscription will not renew. Screenshot it.
6. Watch for the cancellation confirmation email.

If the cancel button is missing or errors, use their Help Center chat and
paste this:

---

Hello,

I would like to request the cancellation of my Coursera Plus subscription,
effective immediately.

Please confirm once this has been processed and that no further charges
will be made.

Thanks,
Simon
