## What counts as waste, ruled 2026-08-25

Waste is money that spends itself. The test: did I decide this, or did it
just happen?

- Not waste: rent, groceries, transport, meds, bills, and planned fun I would
  happily buy again the next morning.
- Waste: delivery or eating out when there was food at home; impulse buys I
  did not want an hour before paying; subscriptions unused in the last month.

Catching a waste before paying means logging its amount in Money Saved.

## How I want to be talked to about money

Blunt, zero sugar. Say the uncomfortable number first. No cushioning.
Honest is not the same as shaming. Name the number, never punish the person.
