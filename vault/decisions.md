**2026-08-25. The business is accessibility checks for businesses, first.**
Simon checks Hamilton business websites for accessibility (AODA is the lever),
sells and delivers by email, all text. Helping disabled people directly with

**2026-08-25. The AI tool serves the business, checks first.**
Simon wants to build an AI tool. Decided: it lives inside the accessibility
business, as a helper that speeds up website checks. Not a separate product
yet. Rule we set: do the work by hand first, automate what repeats. The
bigger dream, AI that helps nonverbal people talk, is noted as the long
game, not dropped, not started.
