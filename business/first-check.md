# Your first check, in 20 minutes

Target: The Burnt Tongue, Hamilton. https://www.theburnttongue.com

This is practice. We do not send them anything. The goal is your first real
sample report, done on a real site.

Do these four tests in Chrome on your computer. Write down what you see in
plain words. Rough notes are fine, I turn them into the report.

## Test 1. The keyboard test (5 min)

Some people cannot use a mouse. They move through a site with the Tab key.

1. Open the site. Click once in the address bar, then press Tab, again and
   again.
2. Watch the page. Can you always SEE which link or button you are on?
   (A visible outline or highlight should move as you press Tab.)
3. Can you reach the menu, every page link, and any order button this way?
4. Write down: where you got lost, anything you could not reach, anywhere
   the highlight disappeared.

## Test 2. Lighthouse (5 min)

Chrome has a free built-in checker.

1. On the site, right click anywhere, click "Inspect".
2. In the panel that opens, find the "Lighthouse" tab. It may hide behind
   the ">>" arrows.
3. Untick everything except "Accessibility". Click "Analyze page load".
4. Write down: the score out of 100, and the list of red items it shows.
   Copy their titles word for word.

## Test 3. The zoom test (3 min)

Many people with low vision zoom everything.

1. Press Ctrl and + until the browser says 200%.
2. Look around. Does text overlap? Does anything disappear? Can you still
   use the menu?
3. Write down anything broken.

## Test 4. The Simon test (5 min)

This one is yours alone.

1. Pretend you want to order food or ask a question.
2. Rule: you cannot make a phone call. Ever.
3. Is there any way? An order link, an email, a form? Or is the phone the
   only door?
4. Write down what you find. If the phone is the only way, that is the
   strongest finding in the whole report, because you are the proof.

## Then

Paste all your notes into our chat, messy is fine. I write them up into
`business/reports/` using the template, and that report becomes the sample
you show every future customer.
